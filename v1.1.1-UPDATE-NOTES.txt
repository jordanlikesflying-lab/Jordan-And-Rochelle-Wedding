Jordan & Rochelle Wedding Manager v1.1.1

RUN:
v1.1.1-rsvp-and-reliability.sql

This migration includes the v1.1.0 invitation-sent/image-search columns too,
so it is safe to use even if v1.1.0 SQL was not run yet.

Changes:
- Public RSVP phone and address fields are no longer required.
- Phone/address labels do NOT say "optional".
- Children's names may be left blank while child_count remains accurate.
- Adult names remain required for attending adults.
- Public RSVP success screen includes a short confirmation number.
- Every new public RSVP now goes to Needs Review, even when it matches an invitation.
- RSVP Review now has Needs Review, All RSVPs, and Reviewed views.
- Added Create RSVP button in RSVP Review for phone/in-person responses.
- Admin-created RSVPs are verified immediately and can link to an invitation.
- Admin-created RSVP counts do not require every child to be named.
- Unnamed children are called out in RSVP detail while still included in totals.
- Sign-in retries transient connection failures up to two additional times.
- Command Center loading retries transient connection failures.
- Keeps all v1.1.0 image search and Invitation Sent features.
