Jordan & Rochelle Wedding Manager v1.0.7

RUN FIRST:
v1.0.7-person-emails.sql

Changes:
1. Household people load reliably with the initial Command Center data.
   You should no longer need to edit a household to make missing people appear.

2. Wedding Jobs can assign multiple people at once.
   - Assign People button on each job
   - search by person name, household, or email
   - grouped by household
   - shows Attending / Not attending / No RSVP yet
   - shows Already assigned
   - select multiple people and save together
   - optional email requests sent individually

3. Each person inside an invitation can have a separate email address.
   Edit Invitation -> People in this household -> Email
   Job requests prefer that individual's email, with household/RSVP email fallback.

No "job completed" status was added.
